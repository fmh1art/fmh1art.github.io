## A short introduction

I am Meihao Fan (范梅浩 in Chinese), currently a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Renmin University of China (RUC). I am a member of [DEKE Lab](http://deke.ruc.edu.cn/) of Renmin University of China and under the supervision of Prof. [Ju Fan](http://iir.ruc.edu.cn/~fanj/) and Prof. Xiaoyong Du. Before that, I completed my B.S. in 2023 at Chongqing Jiaotong University.

My research focuses on **LLM-powered data agents**, **reinforcement learning**, and **post-training for open-source LLMs**. I am interested in building **cost-effective and reliable agentic systems** for data-intensive tasks such as data preparation, entity resolution, and autonomous data science.
